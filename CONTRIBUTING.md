# Contributing

Thank you for helping. Reviews of the guide's content (species identification, nests, calendar, first aid, beekeeping
measures) are especially welcome: open an issue with the text you would change and a source.

## Code contributions

VespaControl is licensed under the AGPL-3.0-or-later, and the copyright holder also offers it under commercial licences
to fund its development. So before a code or content contribution can be merged, contributors are asked to confirm in the
pull request:

> I wrote this contribution (or have the right to submit it), I license it under the AGPL-3.0-or-later (CC BY 4.0 for
> texts and images), and I grant Álvaro Araya a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free right to also distribute
> it under other licences, including commercial ones.

Contributions keep their author credit in the history and in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Rules that apply to every change

See `nightly/NIGHTLY.md`: privacy first, always free, Spanish as the source language, synthetic data only in checks,
and `node nightly/check.mjs` must pass.
